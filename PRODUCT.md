# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People with diabetes (Type 1 or Type 2) managing their own condition independently. They open the app at physiologically-defined moments: before and after meals, at the fasting checkpoint in the morning, sometimes before bed. The interaction is brief and purposeful — fingers already pricked, glucometer reading in hand. Elderly users are a significant segment: vision may be reduced, hands may shake, and patience for friction is low.

## Product Purpose

Minha Glicemia is a personal blood glucose log. It accepts a reading, associates it with a meal type, and makes the history visible — with reminders, statistics, and PDF export. Success is a user who opens the app, logs a value in under 10 seconds, and trusts that the number is saved correctly. The app is not a therapeutic guide, a social platform, or a health coach. It is a precise, reliable instrument for a daily medical routine.

## Brand Personality

Precise, efficient, serious.

Medical-grade credibility without clinical coldness. The tool is trusted the way a well-made glucometer is trusted — it is accurate, it is fast, it gets out of the way. No encouragement banners, no streak celebrations, no ambient noise. Data is the product.

## Anti-references

- **Generic hospital blue**: institutional whites and deep cold blues that signal waiting room, not personal tool
- **Fitness-tracker energy**: gamified badges, streak counters, achievement popups — this is not a workout app
- **Pharmacy app**: cluttered feature grids, promotional content, things the user never asked for
- **Startup health tech**: glossy gradients, abstract blobs, "your health journey" copy that mistakes inspiration for information

## Design Principles

1. **Precision over decoration** — every visual element earns its place through information clarity, not aesthetics. If removing it doesn't cost information, remove it.
2. **Effortless daily ritual** — the most common action (logging a reading) must be zero-friction. The app should feel as fast as a physical glucometer, never slower.
3. **Status communicates, interface recedes** — health status (normal / high / very high) carries meaning through color and data, not through copy, alerts, or explanations. The interface facilitates reading; it does not editorialize.
4. **Designed for real biology** — elderly hands, diminished vision, distracted moments at meals. Text must be readable without glasses. Tap targets must be forgiving. Color alone must never be the sole carrier of critical information.
5. **Serious without being cold** — medical credibility does not require clinical sterility. Warmth lives in legibility, generous spacing, and calm — not in emoji or encouragement.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA as a floor. Elderly users are an explicit audience. Minimum readable text sizes throughout — no 11px labels. High contrast for status colors. Reduced motion respected. Color paired with text labels for all health-status indicators (never color alone).
